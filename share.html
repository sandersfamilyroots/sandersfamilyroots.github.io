Share & Contact

This archive grows because family members send in what they hold — a family sheet, a photograph, a certificate, news of a birth or a passing. If that’s you, you’re in the right place. Pick whichever door fits what you have:

Family Group Sheet

Fill in your family — parents, spouse, and children, with dates and places as you know them. One form covers one marriage; if there was more than one marriage, send one form for each.

Open the form →

Share a Photo or Document

Send a photograph, a certificate, an obituary, a letter, or a family story. Old shoebox finds welcome — if it names a Sanders or kin, the family wants to see it.

Open the form →

Report a Life Event

Tell us about a birth, a marriage, a divorce, a passing, a burial, or military service — one event per form, so nothing gets tangled.

Open the form →

You send it → we check it against the records → it’s added with your name as the source.

Everything sent in is reviewed before it enters the tree, and every fact is credited to the person who shared it. If something you send raises a question, we may write back — that’s how the record stays honest.

Rather just write?

Email the family archive at sandersfamilytree@gmail.com — questions and stories welcome there too.

Spotted a mistake on a page? That counts too — corrections are welcome, and if you hold a document that settles a question, even better.
