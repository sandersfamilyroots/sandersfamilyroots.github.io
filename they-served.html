They Served — Honoring Our Family in Uniform

Some of them answered the call in wartime, some made a career of it, and one gave twenty-four years to the Army. This section gathers every member of the family whose military service the records can show — from a War of 1812 service record and a Confederate private in the 13th Mississippi, through both World Wars, Korea, Vietnam, and Desert Storm, down to our own generation. Seventeen are confirmed so far. Seven more are listed as indicated but not yet proven, and they are labeled as such — in this family, a claim doesn’t get printed as fact until a record carries it. The men who only registered for the draft are named separately below, with respect: registration alone isn’t service, and the list will grow as service records come to light. If you hold a discharge paper, a service photograph, or a story that belongs here, the family wants to see it.

  • Jacob Harty (1765–1840) — War of 1812 service record. The record stands under his name; it may prove to be his son Jacob Jr.’s, and that question stays open in the research.
  • Joseph Pearson Sanders (1831–1927) — Confederate Army, 13th Mississippi Infantry; his pension application file is held by the Mississippi Department of Archives and History.
  • Jacob Menasco (1828–1906) — Civil War service record.
  • Burvee Belvin Ward (1924–2000) — U.S. Army, World War II — enlistment and VA records.
  • Leonard G. Grenier (1925–2012) — U.S. Air Force — service inscribed on his grave marker.
  • Earl Moody Sanders (1923–1977) — U.S. Marine Corps, 1942–1946, then U.S. Army, 1950–1954.
  • Leonard Eugene Sanders (1920–1993) — U.S. Army, World War II; Staff Sergeant; enlisted at Fort Barrancas in 1940.
  • Hebert Auld Schlemmer (1924–1961) — Service recorded in the VA’s veterans gravesite records.
  • Grover Cleveland Sanders (1922–2012) — Private First Class, U.S. Army (VA burial record).
  • Alfred Leon Sanders (1931–2020) — Private First Class, U.S. Army (VA memorial record).
  • Ruben Mizer Sanders Jr. (1931–2003) — Master Sergeant, U.S. Army; Korea and Vietnam; Bronze Star.
  • Jesse Herman Carroll (1935–2010) — Technical Sergeant, U.S. Air Force; Korea- and Vietnam-era service (VA memorial record).
  • Samuel Lee Sanders (1948–2015) — Sergeant First Class, U.S. Army; twenty-four years, including Vietnam and Desert Storm; retired 1993.
  • Robert Thomas Sanders (1942–2025) — U.S. Air Force, 1965–1971.
  • Jerry Everett Sanders (1944–2019) — U.S. Army, Vietnam.
  • Lloyd Yvonne Sanders (1946–2022) — U.S. Army, 68th Maintenance Battalion, Vietnam, 1966–1967 (per his obituary).
  • David Leon Sanders (1957–2026) — U.S. Marine Corps.

Service indicated, not yet proven

  • Joe Lemuel Ward (1950–2002) — A VA memorial page under his exact name records U.S. Army service; the identification is probable, not proven.
  • William Atwell — A candidate 1950 census entry lists a William Atwell serving in the U.S. Army; the match is not yet proven.
  • Joseph Alan Grenier (1958–2019) — His obituary records brief U.S. Navy service as a young man; no service record has surfaced yet.
  • Eddie Ronald Grenier — U.S. Army. Service is indicated by a career profile; no service record has corroborated it yet.
  • Aaron A. Sanders (1932–2020) — His funeral-home obituary records U.S. Air Force service as a Captain, including Operation Dominic in 1962; no service record has corroborated it yet.
  • Donald Leroy Sanders — U.S. Marine Corps — service reported by his daughter Kim; not yet proven by a service record.
  • Thomas Barton Sanders — U.S. Army — service reported by his daughter Kathy; not yet proven by a service record.
